Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

This app ("Postiz Content Planner") is a private tool operated by one individual to schedule and publish their own videos. Contact: duemermaurice@gmail.com

2. What data the app accesses

When the owner connects an account (YouTube, TikTok or Instagram), the app receives an access token from the platform. It uses this token only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to read basic information about the connected channel (for example the channel name and profile picture).

3. What data we store

Access tokens and the content the owner schedules are stored on the owner's own computer. We do not store data about other people. We do not collect data from visitors of this website.

4. How we use data

Data is used only to publish the owner's own content to the connected platforms. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API only to upload videos to the owner's own channel.

6. Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google account security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), in TikTok under "Manage app permissions", and in Instagram under "Apps and websites". Stored tokens can be deleted by removing the connected channel in the app.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: duemermaurice@gmail.com
